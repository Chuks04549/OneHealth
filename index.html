Your health, one app.

OneHealth is a free mobile health tracker with an AI coach that adapts to you — built for people who don't have access to personal trainers or nutritionists.

6 Core features
AI Powered coaching
$0 Cost to users
The Problem

Most health apps either charge a monthly fee or just throw numbers at you with no context. If you don't already know what to do with a calorie count or a macro split, the data doesn't help. I built OneHealth to close that gap — especially for people in communities like mine in the Bronx, where access to nutrition guidance and personal coaching isn't a given.

Features

Everything in one place

No premium tiers. No paywalls. Just the tools people need to take care of themselves.

📸

Photo Meal Logging

Snap a photo of your food. AI breaks it down into calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, and micronutrients — no database searching.

🤖

Eugene — AI Health Coach

An AI assistant that adapts how it talks to you based on your consistency. Been away? It checks in easy. On a streak? It pushes you harder.

🏃

Run & Workout Tracking

Log runs with pace and effort level. Track strength workouts by muscle group. See your progress over time.

👣

Step Counter

Uses your phone's built-in motion sensors to count daily steps — no extra hardware or wearable needed.

📈

Weight & Macro Trends

Charts your weight, calorie intake, and macros over time so you can see patterns, not just daily numbers.

💡

Smart Recommendations & Fun Facts

Personalized suggestions based on what you've actually logged — not generic tips. Plus daily health facts to keep you learning.

Built With
